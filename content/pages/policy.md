At AfroData, we protect your privacy. This policy outlines our data practices.

## Information Collection

* **Personal Information:** We do not collect personal identification information. We do not have user registration or a contact form.
* **Non-Personal Information:** Our analytics service collects non-personal data when you interact with the site, including:
  * Browser name
  * Country of origin (without storing IP addresses)
  * Referring websites
  * Device name
  * This data is used solely to improve user experience and is retained for **90 days**.

## Cookies

We do not use or store cookies.

## External Links & Embedded Content

Our site may include embedded content or links to external websites. We do not control these third-party sites and are not responsible for their privacy practices. 

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent any information to third parties.

## Policy Changes

We may update this policy at any time. Changes take effect immediately upon posting. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the changes.
